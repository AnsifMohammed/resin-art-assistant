2Introduction

2.1Purpose

This document defines what the Saba Art AI Messaging Assistant must do. It is the agreed reference for the client and the development team when designing, building and accepting the system. Each requirement has a unique ID, a priority and a delivery phase so that it can be traced through development and testing.

Priorities: Must required for the phase to be accepted; Should important, delivered in the phase unless agreed otherwise; Could desirable, delivered if time allows.

2.2Scope

The scope covers two connected parts:

  • The AI messaging assistant — receives customer messages from WhatsApp and Instagram, answers routine questions automatically from the business's own information, and hands everything sensitive to a person.
  • The admin and staff dashboard — a web app, installable on a phone, where the owner and helpers read conversations, reply, handle escalations and manage the business information the AI uses.

Work is planned in four phases, as set out in the project proposal. The phase shown against each requirement indicates when it is delivered.

PhaseFocusSummary
1Working demo and setupAI replies to routine questions, hand-over to the owner or helpers, the admin and staff dashboard, knowledge base editing, analytics, audit log and demo mode with simulated customers.
2Go live on WhatsApp and InstagramConnection of the real WhatsApp Business number and Instagram account through Meta (including business verification and app approval), replies to real customers, and alerts by phone notification and email.
3Orders and customer toolsOrder board, payment records, message templates, broadcasts, customer lists and product catalogue.
4Payments and delivery trackingPayment links, shipping and tracking, Instagram comment replies, away message and 24-hour window alerts.

3Business context and objectives

3.1Context

Saba Art is a small handmade resin-art business in Thrissur, Kerala. Customers contact the business on WhatsApp and Instagram. Most messages are repetitive questions — prices, delivery times and charges, product care and custom orders — written in English, Malayalam or Manglish (Malayalam written in English letters). Answering these by hand takes the owner's time away from making products, and customers who message outside working hours wait for a reply.

A smaller number of messages are sensitive: refunds, complaints, payment claims, damaged items and questions about products the business does not list. These must always be handled by a person.

3.2Objectives

  1. Instant replies, 24/7, for routine questions, grounded only in the business's own price list and policies.
  2. Never let the AI handle sensitive matters. Refunds, complaints, payment claims and unknown products go to a person.
  3. One dashboard for the owner and helpers, usable on a phone.
  4. A full audit trail of every reply, escalation and change.

Access principle

Staff see the minimum needed to handle the customer in front of them, and nothing more. Roles are enforced by the backend on every request, using the role in the verified login token. The dashboard hides pages a user cannot use, but this is for convenience only and is never relied on for security.

5System overview

The diagram shows how a customer message travels through the system and how people interact with it.

Channels
Customers use their normal WhatsApp or Instagram app. The business connects a WhatsApp Business number (WhatsApp Cloud API) and an Instagram Professional account (Instagram API with Instagram Login).
Meta webhooks
Meta delivers every new message and event to the assistant, and the assistant sends replies back through Meta's APIs.
Message intake
Checks that each event really comes from Meta, ignores duplicates, detects echoes, stores the message and a copy of any media, and places it in a job queue for processing.
AI decision
After a 10-second batching wait, sends the knowledge base, recent history and the new messages to Google Gemini (model gemini-2.5-flash) through the Gemini API. The result is checked against a strict format, then the business rules are applied.
Auto-reply or escalation
Routine questions are answered automatically. Anything sensitive, unclear or not covered by the knowledge base becomes an escalation for a person.
Dashboard
A web app for the admin and staff, installable on a phone as a PWA, with live updates of new messages and escalations.
Notifications
Push notifications and email alerts tell the admin about new escalations.
Data
A PostgreSQL database holds all records and the job queue. Redis is used for login rate limiting and for caching the knowledge base.

6.1Message intake (INT)

IDRequirementPriorityPhase
FR-INT-01The system shall receive incoming customer messages from WhatsApp and Instagram through Meta webhooks.Must2
FR-INT-02The system shall complete Meta's webhook verification handshake, returning the challenge only when the verify token matches the configured value.Must2

FR-INT-08The system shall keep one customer record per channel. The same person on WhatsApp and on Instagram is treated as two customers.Must1
FR-INT-09The system shall keep one ongoing conversation per customer, holding the full message history.Must1

FR-RUL-05The system shall escalate with reason unapproved_intent any intent that is not in the auto-reply list (price, delivery, care, custom_order).Must1

7Business rules summary

Before the rules run, three checks apply: the conversation must be ai_active and automation not paused (otherwise the message is stored with no reply); the 24-hour window must be open (otherwise window_closed); and the AI output must be valid (otherwise parse_error). The rules are then applied to every AI decision in this order. The first rule that matches decides the outcome.

StepCheckEscalation reasonOutcome
1Message contains an always-escalate phrase (FR-RUL-02)always_escalate_phraseEscalate
2Intent is an always-escalate intent (FR-RUL-03)complaint → intent_complaint
refund → intent_refund
unknown → intent_unknown
payment → payment_claim
off_topic → unapproved_intent		Escalate
3The answer needs facts not in the knowledge base (FR-RUL-04)missing_factsEscalate
4Intent is not price, delivery, care or custom_order (FR-RUL-05)unapproved_intentEscalate
—None of the above—Auto-reply
Note on rule order

Intent is checked before missing facts. A question about a product that is not in the catalogue, which the AI labels unknown, therefore escalates as intent_unknown rather than missing_facts. Either way, the customer receives no automatic answer and a person handles it.

8Conversation state machine

Every conversation is in exactly one of four states. The AI only works on conversations in ai_active.

FromEventToWho
ai_activeA business rule or AI decision requires a personescalatedAI / system
ai_activeAdmin or staff replies from the dashboard, or an echo arrives from the phone appowner_handlingAdmin / staff
escalatedA person replies to the customerowner_handlingAdmin / staff
escalated / owner_handlingConversation marked resolvedai_activeAdmin / staff
Any stateAdmin pauses automationpausedAdmin
pausedAdmin resumes automationai_activeAdmin

All state changes are written to the audit log. Staff cannot pause or resume automation. An echo must switch the conversation to owner_handling before any AI job runs for that conversation.

12Assumptions, constraints and out of scope

12.1Assumptions and constraints

  • Meta business verification and App Review are required before going live. Their timing depends on Meta and is outside the project team's control.
  • The WhatsApp number used for the business must not be registered on the WhatsApp phone app.
  • The Instagram account must be a Professional account.
  • Business-initiated WhatsApp messages outside the 24-hour window require approved template messages, and Meta charges for template messages.
  • The quality of AI replies depends on an accurate, up-to-date knowledge base maintained by the business.

12.2Out of scope (for now)

  • Native apps in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store (the dashboard is installed as a PWA instead).
