Good to know

Many messages, one reply

If a customer sends a few short messages in a row, the assistant waits a moment and answers them together.

Step in any time

If you reply to a customer yourself, the assistant notices and stays out of that chat.

Nothing is lost

Every message and reply is saved, so you can read the whole conversation at any time.

What it handles — and what comes to you

The assistant only answers what it can answer from your own information. Everything else is handed over to you (in technical terms, “escalated”).

Handled automatically

  • Prices of the products on your list
  • Delivery time and charges by area
  • Care instructions
  • Custom-order options for listed products — for example, a name on a clock
  • How many days each product takes to make

Always comes to you

  • Refunds, returns and cancellations
  • Complaints
  • Damaged or wrong items
  • Payment questions, “I’ve paid” or “Where is my order?”
  • Products that are not on your list
  • Anything it isn’t sure about

The assistant never confirms a payment and never makes up a price — if it isn’t in your price list, it hands over to you.

When a chat comes to you

1

You get an alert

A notification on your phone, with an email as a backup, saying which customer needs you and why.

2

The assistant goes quiet

It won’t reply in that chat while you or your helper are handling it.

3

You hand it back

When you mark the chat as done, the assistant looks after that customer again.

Key features

Everything you need to keep customers happy without being glued to your phone — and you can update your price list yourself, any time.

Instant replies, 24/7

Routine questions are answered in seconds — late at night, on Sundays and during festival rush.

Uses only your information

Knows when to hand over

Your helpers can join

Give helpers their own login. They see only the chats handed to them — nothing else.

Dashboard on your phone

Install it on your home screen like an app, and get a notification for every new hand-over.

Pause any time

One switch stops all automatic replies. New messages are still saved for you to answer.

See how it’s doing

Messages received, how many were answered automatically, and how quickly customers got a reply.

Full history

A record of every reply — whether it came from you, a helper or the assistant — and when.

Who sees what

You (the owner) see everything: all chats, your price list, reports, settings and the full history. Helpers see only the chats handed over to them, so customer details stay private.
